# Editorial collage image placeholders

These six PNGs are **original vector illustration placeholders**, retained with their editable SVG originals in the sources folder. The hero is in active use; politics, business, and nations remain available to restore the original supporting illustrations. The coin and figure are retained for reference. These are not photographs or supplied historical artwork.

Keep replacements strictly grayscale. The frontend additionally applies `grayscale(100%) contrast(1.1)`. Use a transparent PNG for cutouts, export in sRGB, and keep the artwork centered with a small breathing margin. Avoid a white rectangle around transparent illustrations.
